# Earning Gold

Gold is earned by playing. It buys [Booster Packs](booster-packs.md).

| Source | Gold |
|---|---|
| Finishing a match, win or lose | 5 |
| Your first win of the day | 50 |
| Completing a daily quest | 60 |

## Matches

Every match you finish pays 5 Gold, whether you win or lose. There is no daily limit.

## First win of the day

Your first win each day pays 50 Gold on top of the match reward. The day resets at midnight UTC.

## Daily quests

You receive one new quest each day and can hold up to three at once. Each quest pays 60 Gold when you complete it. A quest asks for something like winning 2 matches, playing 3 matches as a named class, or playing 15 cards.
