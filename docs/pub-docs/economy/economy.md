# Economy

The King and Queen have decreed three rules for the kingdom's economy. The Council was asked for a fourth and is still deliberating.

## No exclusive power for money

- Every gameplay card is available without paying.
- Spending may accelerate collection progress or buy cosmetics, but it will not grant stronger versions of cards.
- Hero skins and card backs have no gameplay effect.

## Clear pack rules

By royal decree, every price, drop rate, and guarantee is published on the [Booster Packs](booster-packs.md) page.

## A viable free path

The King and Queen wish that a free player build a competitive deck through ordinary play. New player grants, quests, earned currency, and crafting shorten the path to power. [Currency](currency.md) lists every way to earn Gold.
