# Currency

In the kingdom, His Majesty and the Council have approved the use of two currencies: Gold and Gems. Either one buys [Booster Packs](booster-packs.md).

## Gold

Gold is earned by playing.

| Source | Gold |
|---|---|
| Finishing a match, win or lose | 5 |
| Your first win of the day | 50 |
| Completing a daily quest | 60 |

### Matches

Every match you finish pays 5 Gold, whether you win or lose. There is no daily limit. A match you concede, or forfeit by sitting out your turns, pays you nothing.

### First win of the day

Your first win each day in a random match pays 50 Gold on top of the match reward. A win against a friend pays the match reward only. The day resets at midnight UTC.

### Daily quests

The Council posts one new quest for you each day, and you can hold up to three at once. Each quest pays 60 Gold when you complete it. A quest asks for something like winning 2 matches, playing 3 matches as a named class, or playing 15 cards. A match that ends in a concession or a forfeit counts toward no one's quests.

## Gems

Gems are purchasable in the store.
