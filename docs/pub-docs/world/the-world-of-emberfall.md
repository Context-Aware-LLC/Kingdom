# The World of the Kingdom of Bad Ideas

The Kingdom of Bad Ideas takes place after a sky-fire cataclysm. Embers still drift like snow in parts of the realm, and every faction has learned to survive, rebuild, or claim power from what remains.

Its capital is **Pendingham**, the seat of his royal majesty, and the name of the first card set.

The setting favors worn materials, old ruins, practical magic, and the tension between destruction and renewal. Its people are not restoring the old world exactly as it was; they are deciding what should rise in its place.

## The class traditions

- **Emberward** keeps the forge alight: heat, cinders, weapons, and decisive action.
- **Tidecaller** works through tides, frost, mist, and patient control.
- **Gravekeep** honors the dead so the living can continue, guarding gates, bells, and memories.
- **Verdant** brings new growth through ruined stone and turns survival into abundance.
- **Clockwright** applies brass, blueprints, and precision machinery to reconstruction.
- **Nightveil** protects the settlement's edges through secrecy, speed, and carefully chosen strikes.
- **Stonewarden** raises foundations and defenses that endure fire, weather, and siege.
- **Tempest** harnesses storms as power: volatile, dangerous, and capable of driving renewal.

Neutral cards often depict travelers, relics, wardens, creatures, and survivors whose stories cross class boundaries.
