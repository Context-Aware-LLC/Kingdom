# Cards, Decks, and Characters

A match-ready deck belongs to one Character and contains exactly 30 collectible cards.

**Availability note:** dual-class Characters and player-built decks describe the full-game rules. The current vertical slice uses the Emberward/Tidecaller playable pair and prebuilt decks, so every construction feature described here may not yet be exposed in a player build.

## Characters and classes

A Character binds two different classes in an ordered pairing:

- The **primary class** supplies the Character's Hero Power.
- The **secondary class** expands the cards available to the deck.
- Reversing the same two classes creates a different Character because it changes the Hero Power.

A Character's pairing is permanent. A deck may contain cards from either of that Character's classes plus Neutral cards. Cards from every other class are ineligible.

See [Classes](classes.md) for each class identity and Hero Power.

## Deck construction

A legal deck follows all of these rules:

| Rule | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Size | Exactly 30 cards |
| Common, Rare, or Epic copies | No more than 2 copies of one card |
| Legendary copies | No more than 1 copy of one card |
| Class access | Primary class, secondary class, or Neutral only |
| Character | Bound to exactly one Character |

An incomplete or otherwise illegal deck may be kept as a draft, but it is not eligible to enter a match until every rule passes.

## Card types

### Minions

Minions have a mana cost, Attack, Health, and sometimes a tribe, keywords, or triggered effects. They remain on the board until destroyed, returned to hand, or transformed.

New minions normally have summoning sickness and cannot attack that turn. Fervor and Skirmish are the exceptions described in the [Keyword Glossary](keywords.md).

### Spells

Spells perform one or more effects, then leave play. Their text states whether they need a target and what they can affect.

### Weapons

Weapons give the hero Attack and durability. Equipping a new weapon replaces the old one. A hero normally attacks once per turn and takes retaliation damage when striking a minion.

### Hero Powers and tokens

Hero Powers are tied to a Character's primary class. They cost 2 mana and may be used once per turn unless a rule explicitly says otherwise.

Tokens are created by other cards or powers. They are not collectible and cannot be placed in a deck.

## Rarity

Cards may be Common, Rare, Epic, or Legendary. Rarity controls deck copy limits and presentation; it does not grant an automatic rules advantage. Foil or golden treatments are cosmetic versions of the same gameplay card.
