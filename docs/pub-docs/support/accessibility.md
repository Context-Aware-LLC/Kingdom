# Accessibility

We want everyone to be able to play. The game is still in development and has not been tested for accessibility yet, so this page says what we are building toward and how to tell us when we miss.

## What we are building toward

We use the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1, Level AA, as our reference for the game and for the website. In practice that means:

- Text that is large enough to read on a phone, with strong contrast against its background.
- Text that follows your device's text size setting, up to 200%, without cutting off labels.
- Color is never the only signal. A rule shown in color is also shown by shape, text, an icon or motion.
- Buttons and cards large enough to tap, kept clear of notches and rounded corners.
- Anything you can do by hovering with a mouse, you can also do by touch. Anything you can drag, you can also do without dragging.
- Menus that work with a keyboard or a gamepad, with a visible focus indicator.
- A reduced-motion setting that quiets decorative animation without hiding what happened in the match.
- A plain-language reason whenever the game refuses an action.

## Where we are

Kingdom of Bad Ideas is not released yet. Nothing on the list above has been through release testing or an independent audit, and we do not claim conformance with any standard today. We will update this page as each item is tested.

## Tell us what is not working

If something is hard to read, reach or use, write to support@kingdomofbadideas.com. It helps to include your device, how you were playing (touch, mouse, keyboard or gamepad), the screen you were on, and what you were trying to do. A human will reply.
