# Economy Principles

This page states the player-facing rules the economy is expected to honor. It is not a pricing, drop-rate, reward-track, or release announcement; those numbers remain unannounced until they are finalized and published in the game.

## No exclusive power for money

- Every gameplay card must be earnable without paying.
- Spending may accelerate collection progress or buy cosmetics, but it must not grant a stronger version of a card.
- No gameplay card may be exclusive to premium currency.
- Foil or golden cards are cosmetic versions of the same gameplay card.
- Hero skins and card backs have no gameplay effect.

## Clear pack rules

If randomized packs are sold, their contents and odds must be published. A pack bought with earned currency and the same pack bought with premium currency must use the same card pool and odds.

Duplicate protection and crafting should ensure that surplus cards retain value and that players have a direct path toward a specific card rather than relying only on random openings.

## A viable free path

The progression model is intended to let a free player build a competitive deck through ordinary play. New-player grants, quests, earned currency, and crafting should shorten the path to a first playable collection rather than withholding the basic game.

If live data shows that a free player's path is too slow, acquisition speed and rewards should change before card power does.
