Stonewarden

Built a wall, then built a second wall to protect the first one.

How Stonewarden plays

Stonewarden builds a wall and stands behind it for as long as it takes. Bulwark minions must be attacked first, Health buffs make them very hard to remove, and once the wall is finished the heavy minions walk through whatever is left.

Bulwark
Enemies have to attack a Bulwark minion before anything else, and Earthwall adds another one every turn.
Thicker walls
Buffs add Health and Aegis, which cancels the first damage a minion would take.
Overwhelm
The big finishers carry their leftover damage through a blocker and into the enemy hero.

Hero power

Earthwall

Summon a 0/3 Stone Shard.

Costs 2 mana. Usable once per turn.
