One of eight heroes

Gravekeep

Keeps the cemetery, and is concerned by how many residents keep leaving it.

How Gravekeep plays

Gravekeep treats a minion's death as the start of its career. Its minions leave something behind when they die, some of them get back up, and a few cards will sacrifice one of your own to collect the benefit early.

Toll
A Toll effect happens when the minion dies: a card, some Health, a fresh pair of Shades. Losing a fight is often the plan.
Sacrifice
Spend a friendly minion to draw cards, summon more, or take an enemy minion for yourself.
They come back
Rekindle returns a minion once with 1 Health, and the largest spells empty the graveyard back onto the board.

Hero power

Weaken

Give a minion -1/+0.

Costs 2 mana. Usable once per turn.
