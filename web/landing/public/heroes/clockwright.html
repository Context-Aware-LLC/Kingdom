How Clockwright plays

Clockwright builds a machine out of small parts. Gear Wisps arrive one at a time, every Construct on the board makes the others bigger, and the hero power keeps your hand full while the engine warms up.

Many small parts
Card after card summons a 1/1 Gear Wisp. One of them is a rounding error. Six of them are a workforce.
Constructs improve Constructs
Buffs that reach every friendly Construct at once turn a wide board into a tall one.
Never out of ideas
Blueprint draws a card every turn, and a few spells pick a minion back up so it can be played again.
