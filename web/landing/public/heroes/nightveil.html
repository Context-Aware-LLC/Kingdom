Nightveil

Nobody saw Nightveil arrive. Three complaints about it have been filed anyway.

How Nightveil plays

Nightveil picks its fights. Shrouded minions cannot be attacked or targeted until they strike, Venom destroys any minion it damages, and anything too large to kill is sent back to its owner's hand.

Shroud
Enemies cannot attack or target a Shrouded minion until it attacks. You decide when the fight starts.
Venom
Any minion damaged by Venom is destroyed, whatever its size.
Removal on request
Destroy effects, returns to hand, and the occasional enemy minion persuaded to change sides.

Hero power

Backstab

Deal 2 damage to the enemy hero and give all friendly Shroud minions +1/+0.

Costs 2 mana. Usable once per turn.
