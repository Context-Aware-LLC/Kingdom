One of eight heroes

Tempest

Treats the weather as a suggestion. The suggestion is usually lightning.

How Tempest plays

Tempest is the fastest class in the kingdom and the least predictable. Its minions attack twice, its lightning picks its own targets, and its strongest cards borrow mana from next turn without asking.

Doublestrike
A Doublestrike minion attacks twice each turn.
Lightning goes where it likes
Much of the damage lands on a random enemy. It is cheap for what it does, and now and then it is exactly what you wanted.
Backdraft
Strong now, expensive later: a Backdraft card locks some of your Mana Crystals on your next turn.

Hero power

Chain Spark

Deal 1 damage twice to random enemies.

Costs 2 mana. Usable once per turn.
