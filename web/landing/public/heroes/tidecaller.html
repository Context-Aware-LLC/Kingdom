Tidecaller

Summons the tide twice a day, which the tide was already doing without help.

How Tidecaller plays

Tidecaller is in no hurry. It freezes attackers where they stand, drowns large minions down to size, sends the rest back to their owner's hand, and wins with whatever is still standing.

Frost
A frozen character misses its next chance to attack.
Drown
Drown deals damage equal to half of a character's current Health, rounded up. The larger the minion, the worse it goes.
Return to sender
Minions go back to their owner's hand, to be paid for a second time.

Hero power

Tidal Ward

Summon a 1/2 Tide Wisp.

Costs 2 mana. Usable once per turn.
