Keyword Glossary

Keywords compress recurring rules into a short name. If a card's full text conflicts with this glossary, the card's specific text takes priority.

Keyword Rule
Aegis The first instance of damage this minion would take is prevented, then Aegis is removed. It does not stop destroy or silence effects.
Arcane Surge Your damage-dealing spells deal 1 extra damage for each friendly Arcane Surge in play.
Backdraft (N) Locks N of your Mana Crystals on your next turn.
Bulwark While you control a Bulwark minion, enemies must attack it before other legal targets.
Doublestrike This character may attack twice each turn.
Drown Deals damage equal to half the target minion's Health, rounded up. Deals 2 to a hero. Ignores Aegis.
Fervor This minion may attack any legal target on the turn it is played.
Frost A frosted character loses its next opportunity to attack, then thaws.
Lifedrain Damage dealt by this source also restores that much Health to its controller's hero.
Overwhelm When this minion attacks a minion, Attack beyond the defender's remaining Health carries through to the enemy hero.
Rekindle The first time this minion dies, it returns with 1 Health. It keeps its printed keywords but loses enchantments.
Shroud Enemies cannot target or attack this minion until it attacks. Friendly effects may still target it.
Skirmish This minion may attack enemy minions, but not the enemy hero, on the turn it is played.
Toll The listed effect happens when this minion dies.
Vanguard This minion deals its combat damage first. If that damage destroys the defender, the defender deals no retaliation damage.
Venom Any minion damaged by this source is destroyed after damage resolves. Heroes are unaffected by Venom.

Silence

Silence is an effect rather than a standing keyword. It removes a minion's keywords, rules text, triggered effects, and enchantments, leaving its base stats.

Important interactions

  • An Aegis-protected hit deals no damage, so damage riders such as Lifedrain, Venom, and Frost do not trigger from that hit.
  • Fervor permits attacks against any legal target immediately; Skirmish permits only minion attacks immediately.
  • Shroud restricts enemy targeting. It does not protect against effects that do not target a specific character.
