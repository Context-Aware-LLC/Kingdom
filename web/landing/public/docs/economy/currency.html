Economy

Currency

In the kingdom, His Majesty and the Council have approved the use of two currencies: Gold and Gems. Either one buys Booster Packs.

Gold

Gold is earned by playing.

Source Gold
Finishing a match, win or lose 5
Your first win of the day 50
Completing a daily quest 60

Matches

Every match you finish pays 5 Gold, whether you win or lose. There is no daily limit.

First win of the day

Your first win each day pays 50 Gold on top of the match reward. The day resets at midnight UTC.

Daily quests

The Council posts one new quest for you each day, and you can hold up to three at once. Each quest pays 60 Gold when you complete it. A quest asks for something like winning 2 matches, playing 3 matches as a named class, or playing 15 cards.

Gems

Gems are purchasable in the store.
