FAQ

Answers from the Royal Council, which has agreed to be accurate for once. The game is still in development, so details may change before launch.

Early Access

When does Early Access open?

We have not announced a date. Join the Early Access list and we will write to you when it opens, and not before.

Which devices will it run on?

iOS and Android are coming first. The game is built mobile-first and designed to run on desktop later.

What do you do with my email address?

We use it only to tell you about Early Access and launch. We send a confirmation first, every message has an unsubscribe link, and we never sell it. The details are in the Privacy Policy.

I signed up but never got the confirmation email.

Check your spam folder first. The email comes from hello@kingdomofbadideas.com. If it is not there, sign up again from the home page, or write to support@kingdomofbadideas.com.

How it plays

What kind of game is it?

A turn-based collectible card game for two players. You build a 30-card deck, spend mana to play cards and attack, and win by reducing the opposing hero from 30 Health to 0.

Is it free to play?

Final pricing is not announced. Our stated principles are that every gameplay card must be earnable without paying, spending must never buy a stronger version of a card, and no gameplay card is exclusive to premium currency.

What are the heroes and classes?

There are eight: Emberward, Clockwright, Gravekeep, Nightveil, Stonewarden, Tempest, Tidecaller and Verdant. Each has its own style and opinions about the other seven. See a few of the cards.

Online play

Do I need an internet connection?

Yes. Every match is played against a real opponent and judged by our server, so there is no offline mode.

How long do I get on my turn?

75 seconds, with a warning at 15 seconds left. The mulligan gets 45 seconds. A single timed-out turn is not a loss. Two in a row, and you forfeit.

What if I lose my connection mid-match?

The match keeps going and your turns time out until you return. If you reconnect while the match still exists, you will land back in the current game. If both players disconnect, the match is voided, with no winner, no loser and no ranking change.

Can the other player see my hand?

No. The server sends your opponent only card backs and the size of your hand. It never sends your deck order to anyone. The server, not your device, decides damage, targets and random results.

Follow the steps on the delete account page. Your personal data is deleted within 30 days.
