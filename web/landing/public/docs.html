Docs

Kingdom of Bad Ideas is a turn-based card game for two players. You build a 30-card deck and outplay one opponent at a time.

The kingdom is always growing and subject to the whims of His Majesty and the Queen. These pages describe the current rules, which may change.

Gameplay

  • How to Play: match setup, turns, mana, combat and victory.
  • Cards and Decks: card types and deck-building rules.
  • Keywords: what each word printed on a card does.
  • Timers and Reconnection: turn clocks and what happens when a player disconnects.

Heroes

  • The Eight Heroes: each one's style, keywords and Hero Power.

Economy

  • Economy: the promises that govern cards and purchases.
  • Currency: how to earn Gold and get Gems.
  • Booster Packs: prices, drop rates, guarantees and starter packs.
