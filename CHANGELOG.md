# Changelog

All notable changes to Kingdom of Bad Ideas are documented here. Format follows
[Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/).

## [0.0.23] - 2026-11-21

### Added
- Heroes now make a sound when they attack, matched to their weapon: a blunt thud, a blade
  slash or a quick swipe. Unarmed heroes punch with the blunt sound.
- Nightveil's hero power has its own sound.

### Changed
- A balance pass across 57 cards: many are a little weaker, in line with cards around them.
- Throneless King is now a Gravekeeper card instead of a neutral one. The prebuilt deck that
  used him plays Aric, First Among Many instead.
- Sealed Gate Keeper is now a rare 2/3 with Bulwark.
- Renamed cards: Primal Mend is now Primal Might, Rekindled Flame is Stoked Flame, Venom Lash
  is Coup de Grâce, Aegis Templar is Templar of the Ward, Slagshell Bulwark is Slagshell Hulk,
  Doublestrike Hawk is Scissorwing Hawk, Ironclad Bulwark is Ironclad Bastion, Vanguard Drake
  is Spearhead Drake, Restored Bulwark is Mended Golem, and Vanguard Conscript is Eager
  Conscript.
- Glacial Echo copies a minion that costs 4 or less (was 5 or less), and its German text
  says so.

### Fixed
- Dragging a minion again while its attack was still on its way no longer queues extra
  attacks.
- A hero whose weapon broke or was returned to hand no longer plays that weapon's sound on
  later attacks.

### Removed
- Static Shock, Venom Asp, Salvaged Golem, Fade Out, Mass Recall, Deep Channel and Mechanical
  Essence are out of the card pool for now.
